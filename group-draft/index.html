Quantum AI Lab

The group of Juan Carrasquilla at the Institute for Theoretical Physics, ETH Zurich.

We work where computational quantum many-body physics meets machine learning. Much of our work develops neural-network quantum states and variational Monte Carlo for ground states, excited states, finite temperature and real-time dynamics. We also design algorithms for quantum computers.
